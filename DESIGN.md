# Crew & Crop

Direction authorized by the owner: a cozy farming game inspired by Hay Day, with original volumetric Three.js models and characters. An orthographic orbit camera presents a large farm diorama; pitched roofs, rounded foliage, articulated farmers and soft shadows establish depth. HTML order controls remain readable outside the 3D world. The farm is the focal point, with a practical order notebook beside it rather than a dashboard.

ENERGY 3 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2. Movement communicates travel and work. Reduced motion removes decorative bobbing. Green vegetation, brown timber and cream paper form the core palette; terracotta marks the primary order action. Crop gold belongs to ripe crop state. Serif headings recall a farm notebook; system sans keeps instructions readable. Rounded drawing shapes represent crops and characters, squared input edges distinguish tools. Shadows ground objects, rather than elevate panels. Orders are read by Claude on the server when configured, otherwise by a labelled keyword dry run; every reply shows its source.

## Expanded farm reference

The owner supplied four detailed 3D farm references and asked for a close recreation. Selected: the first farm image after the app screenshot, showing a cream house and red barn at the back, golden crop field and mixed vegetable beds at the front-left, chicken yard on the left, cattle and sheep pens on the right, a pond with a footbridge at the front-right, fruit trees, flowers, hay and a cart. Recreate that composition with actual 3D geometry. Increase the terrain footprint from about 23×16 to 32×23 world units and use an orthographic camera for the reference's isometric proportions. Dense crop clusters, roof trim, timber fences, wall cladding, barrels and garden flowers replace the sparse prior scene. Hide place labels to keep the reference composition clear; character labels and speech remain readable. Blue pond and backdrop are scene-specific colours, distinct from the restrained form palette. Scenic animals and vegetable beds are labelled as scenery until gameplay supports them.

## Large farm layout

The owner requested a substantially larger layout with much more content. Extend the terrain to a 65×48 footprint (about four times the previous bounding area), keeping the working farmyard at the centre. Add western grain and vegetable fields, a northern fruit orchard and greenhouses, a farm village with picnic tables, silos and a tractor workshop, eastern livestock paddocks, and a southern lake, stream, footbridge, dock and flower garden. Connect districts with dirt roads and add timber fences, trees, crates, barrels, hay and lanterns. Keep building and character scale unchanged so the expansion is actual new land rather than enlarged props. Camera presets provide the entire overview and six district views; closer presets maintain readable detail on phones. Static crops and livestock in the new districts are scenic; the existing twelve playable crop cells and three lumber trees retain their state. Existing instancing batches handle repeated static details.

The order panel uses the same cream paper, dark green ink and terracotta action colour as the farm. A short status line under the heading states whether live AI or dry run is active and which models are used.
